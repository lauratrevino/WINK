WINK ("What I Need to Know") is an academic support platform for university students. This page explains what information we collect, how it's used, and how it's protected.

WINK Is Independently Developed and Operated

WINK is not owned, operated, or hosted by UTEP. It was independently designed and built, and is solely administered by Dr. Laura Trevino, who is both WINK's developer/administrator and, for the associated research study, the investigator.

As WINK's administrator, Dr. Trevino can access information students enter into or generate through WINK — including uploaded documents, questions/prompts, AI responses, deadlines, practice activity, and grade-calculator information — at any time throughout the semester, for any user. This is a standing administrative capability, not something limited to or triggered by the research study described below. WINK is not an anonymous or private communication channel.

Operational/administrative access and research use are separate concepts. Only data covered by the applicable IRB-approved consent or authorization is used for research purposes; ordinary administrative access exists independently of that and is not limited by it.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, university email address, and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text).
  • Academic profile: major and classification, provided to personalize support.
  • Uploaded documents: course materials (e.g. syllabi) you upload, used to power calendar, chat, and study features.
  • Usage data: questions asked, pages viewed, and similar activity, used to operate and improve the platform.

How We Use Email

We send transactional email only — account verification and password reset messages triggered directly by your actions. We do not send marketing email, and we do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate the WINK platform for you.
  • To send account-related transactional email (verification, password reset).
  • To improve WINK's accuracy and features during the pilot phase.
  • To evaluate how students use AI-supported academic tools and conduct research related to artificial intelligence and student success.

Research Data

This is a small research pilot — currently fewer than 10 students — studying how AI-supported academic tools affect student success. User interactions with WINK, including questions and AI-generated responses, will be recorded and analyzed for research purposes. Access to research data is limited to Dr. Laura Trevino and the small number of research team members who need it to conduct the study, and it is kept on secured, access-controlled systems. Data will be anonymized before being used in research publications or presentations, and reporting will not intentionally identify individual students.

Uploaded Information

Users should upload only information they are authorized to provide. Students should not upload passwords, Social Security numbers, financial account information, medical records, or other highly sensitive information unnecessary for WINK's academic functions.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. WINK relies on a small number of third-party providers to operate: Render hosts the application and its database; Anthropic processes chat prompts and course-document excerpts to generate AI responses (Anthropic's commercial terms state this data is not used to train its models); and Amazon Web Services (AWS) is used for transactional email delivery. Information is shared with these providers only as necessary to provide WINK's functionality.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information maintained by WINK. However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention, Deletion & Anonymization

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, it takes effect immediately: your name and email are replaced with a non-identifying participant label, your password is invalidated, and you're logged out and can no longer sign in. Your activity data — documents, conversations, and similar records — is retained in this de-identified form rather than being deleted outright, so it can continue to support the research study described above, consistent with the consent given at registration.

This pilot involves a small number of participants over a limited period. WINK does not plan to retain data at this scope or duration on an ongoing basis beyond the pilot.

Withdrawing From the Research Study

Deleting your account (available from your Dashboard) is how you withdraw: it immediately and permanently removes your name and email from your record and disables your login. As described above, your already-collected activity data is retained in de-identified form rather than deleted outright — there is currently no separate mechanism to withdraw participation while continuing to use WINK, or to remove already-collected data entirely rather than anonymizing it. If you have questions about this before deciding, reach out using the contact information below.

Accuracy of AI Responses

Information generated by artificial intelligence may contain errors. Users should verify important academic information using official course materials, instructors, advisors, or university systems.

Children

WINK is designed for college students and is not intended for children under 13.
