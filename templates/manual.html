Start Here

Your first few minutes with WINK

Create your account

Enter your name, university, school email, classification, major, and password. Read and accept the WINK Terms of Agreement before finishing registration.

Sign in

Use your email and password from the WINK landing page. If you forget your password, choose Forgot password? and follow the reset instructions.

Best first move: Upload your syllabi and course calendars. The more accurate course material WINK has, the more useful your answers, deadlines, study tools, and grade information can be.
🏠

Dashboard

Your semester at a glance

Your Dashboard is your home base. Use it to see what is due, what is happening today, jump to major WINK tools, and check your profile.

What’s DueSee upcoming academic work WINK has found.
TodaySee the schedule and items that matter today.
Busy Stretch AheadWhen WINK detects a real cluster of deadlines, it can flag the busy period for you.
📚

My Documents

This is how WINK learns your courses

Upload the materials WINK should use when answering course-specific questions. Add the course name and CRN, choose the document type, then select your file.

Supported filesPDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text, PNG and JPG files.
Organized by courseYour document library groups your files by class and lets you search by course or filename.
Manage your filesOpen, download, or delete uploaded documents whenever needed.
Keep files current. If your instructor changes a due date, policy, assignment, or calendar, upload the new version. WINK can only work from what you give it.
💬

Ask WINK

Your AI academic coach

Ask WINK questions about your courses, deadlines, assignments, study needs, or campus resources. WINK uses your uploaded course material first when it is relevant.

🎤 Speak your questionUse the microphone button when your browser supports speech recognition.
🔊 Read answers aloudUse read-aloud to have the latest WINK answer spoken to you.
🔠 Adjust text sizeChat includes text-size controls for easier reading.
＋ Upload from ChatAdd a file permanently to My Documents or use a file only for that conversation.
Recent ChatsReopen recent conversations or start fresh with + New Chat.
👍 FeedbackRate WINK answers with thumbs up or thumbs down so the system can be evaluated and improved.

Try asking:

  • “What do I have due next week?”
  • “Summarize my syllabi.”
  • “What should I study for my upcoming exam?”
  • “Help me build a study plan for this week.”
  • “Where can I get tutoring?”
Check important information. AI can make mistakes. Verify deadlines, grading policies, and important requirements against your instructor or official course materials.
📅

Calendar

Turn course material into an academic plan

WINK can display deadlines found in your course materials. Course colors help you see which class each item belongs to.

  • Confirm an AI-detected deadline that is correct.
  • Correct the title or date if WINK found the right item but got something wrong.
  • Dismiss something that is not actually a deadline.
  • Mark items complete as you finish them.
  • Add and edit your own personal calendar items, including recurring items when available.
🧠

Practice & Review

Turn your course material into study tools

Choose a course and let WINK generate study material from what you uploaded.

  • Flashcards
  • Review questions
  • Practice quizzes with automatic grading and explanations
  • Study summaries
  • Assessment-style quizzes when supported by your uploaded materials
You can attach a handout just for one practice session without permanently saving that handout to your account. Previously answered questions can also return later for review.
🧮

Grade Calculator

See where you stand and what you need

Pick a course and ask WINK to pull the grading breakdown from an uploaded syllabus. Enter your scores to calculate your current grade and explore what you need on remaining work to reach a target grade.

If WINK cannot find the grading weights, you can add or adjust the grading categories yourself.
📈

My Progress

See the habits you are building

My Progress turns your WINK activity into an easy-to-read picture of your academic habits. It is designed to show your own behavior over time, not give you a grade or a “success score.”

  • Active days and questions asked
  • Tracked work completed and on-time completion when WINK has enough information
  • Organization and planning activity
  • Help-seeking and practice activity
  • Week-by-week growth patterns
  • WINK noticed… observations about changes in your habits
🌎

Talk to WINK in Your Language

WINK can match the language you use in Chat

WINK auto-detects the language you type in. Just type your message to WINK in your language and WINK will try to answer in that same language — you can even switch languages from one message to the next, and WINK will follow along.

ArabicChinese (Mandarin)EnglishFilipino (Tagalog)FrenchGermanGujaratiHaitian CreoleHindiItalianJapaneseKoreanPersian (Farsi)PolishPortuguesePunjabiRussianSomaliSpanishSwahiliThaiTurkishUkrainianUrduVietnamese
Good to know: Language auto-detection controls WINK’s Chat replies only. It does not translate the buttons, menus, or other pages in the WINK interface.

Profile & Account

Keep your WINK information current

Your Dashboard profile lets you review and edit information such as your name, classification, major, university, and preferred reply language. You may also see email-verification controls if your address still needs to be confirmed.

🔐

Privacy & Research

Know what WINK records

WINK is being evaluated as a research project. The registration agreement explains the information collected, including account/profile information, course information, uploaded materials, questions and messages, WINK responses, and usage/interaction information.

Read the current Privacy Policy and Terms of Agreement inside WINK for the official description of collection, research use, and anonymization.

Quick Troubleshooting

Try these before declaring WINK possessed 👻

WINK gave an old deadlineCheck whether the newest syllabus/calendar is uploaded. Replace outdated course material.
WINK cannot find course informationMake sure the correct course file is in My Documents and that the course/CRN information is correct.
Microphone is missingYour browser may not support speech recognition or microphone permission may be blocked.
Wrong reply languageCheck Preferred reply language in your Dashboard profile, or choose Auto-detect.
Forgot passwordReturn to the landing page and use Forgot password? under Sign In.
Something looks stuckRefresh the page and try again. If the problem continues, report exactly what you clicked and what happened.
