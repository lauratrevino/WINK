4. What do I need on the rest?

Your final grade is a weighted average — each category counts toward it based on its weight, not equally. So a 60% on a category worth 5% barely moves your grade, while a 60% on a category worth 40% moves it a lot. Here's the idea: every category you've already entered a score for has already "banked" some points toward your final grade. Whatever's left of your final grade is riding entirely on the categories you haven't scored yet — so to hit your goal, you need to know what average you'd need across just those remaining categories. That's what this section figures out for you.
