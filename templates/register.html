WINK Terms of Agreement

Please read this information before creating your account. (View the full Terms of Use and Privacy Policy in a new tab.)

WINK Research Pilot

WINK is an experimental AI-powered student success platform being evaluated as part of a research project. Access is provided free of charge during the Fall 2026 pilot period, currently scheduled from August 17, 2026 through December 20, 2026.

WINK is not owned, operated, or hosted by UTEP. It is independently developed and operated by Dr. Laura Trevino, who is both WINK's developer/administrator and the study's investigator. As administrator, Dr. Trevino can access what you enter into or generate through WINK — including uploads, questions, AI responses, deadlines, practice activity, and grade-calculator information — at any time, for any user. This administrative access applies regardless of whether you agree to the research participation checkbox below — it is a separate thing from the research use described under "Research Data."

Instructor Role & Voluntary Participation

Dr. Trevino is the instructor of record for this course section, in addition to being WINK's developer/administrator and the study's principal investigator. To reduce any pressure to participate, recruitment and consent for the research study are handled by a teaching assistant rather than by Dr. Trevino directly. Participating, declining, or later withdrawing has no effect on your grades, course standing, relationship with the instructor, or access to any standard university resource.

Your Responsibility

WINK is an assistance tool. Students remain responsible for their academic work, decisions, deadlines, registrations, submissions, attendance, and compliance with course and university requirements. Important information should be verified using official course or university sources.

Artificial Intelligence

AI systems can make mistakes. WINK responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or based on an incorrect interpretation of uploaded materials.

Information We Collect

WINK Collects and Stores information about your use of the system, including:
  • Account and profile information you provide
  • Course and academic information
  • Files and course materials you upload to WINK
  • Questions, prompts, and messages you submit to WINK
  • Responses generated by WINK
  • Information you enter into WINK features
  • Usage and interaction information needed to operate and evaluate WINK

How Information Is Used

Information is used operationally to provide WINK itself — to authenticate your account, answer your questions, process uploaded materials, track deadlines, and calculate your progress. WINK also relies on third-party technology providers (including artificial intelligence, hosting, storage, and email services) to operate, so some information is processed by those providers as part of delivering WINK's functionality.

Research Data

Separately from that operational use, your interactions with WINK — including questions and AI-generated responses — will be recorded and analyzed for research purposes. Access to research data is limited to Dr. Laura Trevino and the small number of research team members who need it to conduct the study, and it is kept on secured, access-controlled systems. Data will be anonymized before being used in research publications or presentations, and reporting will not intentionally identify individual students.

If you later delete your account, that's how you'd withdraw: your name and email are immediately replaced with a non-identifying label and you're logged out for good, but your already-collected activity data is retained in this de-identified form to support the study rather than being deleted outright. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Uploaded Content and Privacy

Upload only materials you are authorized to provide. Do not upload passwords, Social Security numbers, financial account information, medical records, or other highly sensitive information that is unnecessary for WINK's academic functions.

Academic Integrity

You are responsible for following your institution's and instructors' academic-integrity requirements. WINK should not be used for cheating, plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, or other prohibited conduct.

Agreement

By continuing, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these terms and the WINK privacy disclosures and agree to the collection and use of information described above.
