WINK My Progress + Research Analytics build

Added:
- Student My Progress page at /progress
- My Progress navigation item
- Dashboard My Progress summary card
- Transparent measures for organization, planning, help-seeking, engagement, practice, and weekly trends
- WINK-generated observations based only on recorded behavior
- Anonymized admin endpoint /research-progress-data
- Research Progress tab in Analytics with participant-level and weekly longitudinal data
- Event logging for practice attempts, quiz answers, and study-plan views

Methodology:
- No single "success score"
- No risk prediction
- No causal claim
- On-time completion is only reported when a completion timestamp is actually recorded
- Research endpoint returns participant codes rather than names/emails
